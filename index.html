17-Year-Old Shot and Killed in Coney Island Housing

Two suspects were taken into custody after a fatal afternoon shooting in Brooklyn.

By News Reporter Brooklyn, NY Updated

Shamar Simons Photo of Shamar Simons — Simons, 17, was identified as the victim of a fatal shooting that occurred Tuesday afternoon at the Coney Island Houses complex in Brooklyn. (NYC Daily Report)

● Key Facts at a Glance

  • Victim: Shamar Simons, 17 years old, Brooklyn resident
  • Incident time: Approximately 2:40 p.m., Tuesday, May 27, 2026
  • Location: Coney Island Houses, Brooklyn, New York
  • Suspect 1: Elijah Bennett, a.k.a. "Leeky Ashton" — in custody
  • Suspect 2: Kareem Brooks, a.k.a. "Nunu Hound" — in custody
  • Weapon: A firearm was recovered at the scene, investigators allege
  • Alleged motive: Investigators believe incident tied to gang dispute — active line of inquiry
  • Lead investigation: Brooklyn North Homicide Squad & NYPD Gang Division
  • Case status: Active — charges pending; investigation ongoing

A 17-year-old boy was shot and killed Tuesday afternoon inside the Coney Island Houses complex in Brooklyn, according to the New York City Police Department. The victim, identified by authorities as Shamar Simons, was struck by gunfire at approximately 2:40 p.m. and was pronounced dead at a nearby hospital shortly thereafter. Photos of Shamar Simons shown above and further below — provided to NYC Daily Report — depict the 17-year-old Brooklyn resident who was fatally wounded in the incident. The shooting sent shockwaves through the densely populated public housing development and drew an immediate law enforcement response from multiple units across Brooklyn.

According to the NYPD, officers from the 60th Precinct were dispatched to the Coney Island Houses complex following a series of 911 calls reporting shots fired in the area. Upon arrival, responding officers located Simons in a common outdoor area of the development, suffering from a gunshot wound. Emergency medical personnel from the New York City Fire Department administered first aid at the scene before transporting him via ambulance to a hospital in the vicinity, where he was later pronounced dead. He was 17 years old.

Timeline of Events

Based on information gathered by law enforcement and reviewed by NYC Daily Report, the following sequence of events reflects the preliminary account established by investigators. Officials cautioned that the timeline may be refined as additional evidence is processed and witness accounts are further corroborated.

At approximately 2:36 p.m., surveillance cameras in the vicinity of the Coney Island Houses complex captured movement consistent with a confrontation taking shape in an outdoor common area of the development, according to sources familiar with the investigation. By 2:40 p.m., multiple 911 calls had been placed from the area reporting shots fired. Officers from the 60th Precinct arrived on the scene at approximately 2:44 p.m. — within four minutes of the first call received.

EMS personnel transported Simons to a hospital at approximately 2:51 p.m. He was pronounced dead at 3:18 p.m., officials confirmed. Brooklyn North Homicide Squad detectives assumed lead jurisdiction over the investigation by approximately 4:30 p.m. Within hours, investigators had identified two individuals believed to be directly connected to the shooting. Elijah Bennett, known as "Leeky Ashton," was taken into custody at approximately 6:15 p.m. Kareem Brooks, known as "Nunu Hound," was apprehended separately at approximately 7:40 p.m., according to law enforcement sources.

Surveillance Footage Released

Investigators have obtained and are actively reviewing surveillance footage recorded in and around the Coney Island Houses complex at the time of the incident. An image derived from that footage, displayed below, depicts the outdoor area near where the shooting is believed to have occurred — captured at approximately the same time authorities allege the confrontation took place. The footage is being processed as a key component of the ongoing investigation.

Officials declined to describe in specific detail what the footage depicted, citing the active nature of the investigation and the potential impact on forthcoming legal proceedings. The NYPD's Crime Scene Unit processed the surrounding area in the hours following the incident, collecting physical evidence that is being analyzed at the department's forensic laboratory.

Police Response

Two individuals identified as Elijah Bennett, known by the street name "Leeky Ashton," and Kareem Brooks, known as "Nunu Hound," were apprehended by police in connection with the fatal shooting. Both men were taken into custody within hours of the incident, according to law enforcement sources familiar with the matter. Formal charges were expected to be filed in the coming days, pending a review by the Brooklyn District Attorney's office and the completion of the NYPD's ongoing investigative proceedings.

"We moved quickly to identify and apprehend the individuals believed to be responsible for this senseless act. A young life has been cut short, and we are committed to pursuing every avenue of justice for his family."

— NYPD spokesperson, statement issued Tuesday evening

Detectives from the Brooklyn North Homicide Squad assumed lead jurisdiction over the investigation. The crime scene was secured and canvassed for ballistic evidence. According to investigators, a firearm believed to be connected to the incident was recovered in the vicinity of the crime scene shortly after officers arrived. Police allege the weapon had been recently discharged at the time of recovery, though authorities noted that its precise role in the shooting remains subject to ongoing forensic analysis and has not been formally established.

A canine unit assisted detectives in tracking a route allegedly used by one of the suspects following the shooting, law enforcement sources said. Nearby surveillance footage from multiple camera positions was obtained and is being reviewed in conjunction with ballistic and forensic evidence collected at the scene. Witnesses present during or shortly after the shooting were identified and interviewed; detectives requested that any additional individuals with relevant information come forward voluntarily.

Witness Statements

Several residents of the Coney Island Houses who were present in the vicinity at the time of the shooting spoke with investigators and, in some cases, with members of the press on Tuesday. The accounts, while varying in specific detail, collectively described a chaotic and rapidly unfolding situation that left bystanders frightened and shaken.

One individual, who declined to be identified by name out of safety concerns, told NYC Daily Report they heard multiple gunshots in quick succession before seeing people scatter from the outdoor courtyard area. "It happened fast," the person said. "By the time anyone knew what was going on, it was already over." Another resident described observing emergency services arrive and a young man being loaded into an ambulance as neighbors watched from a distance.

"It happened fast. By the time anyone knew what was going on, it was already over."

— Witness, Coney Island Houses resident (identity withheld for safety)

Detectives are continuing to canvass the area and have urged anyone with additional information — including video recorded on personal devices in the vicinity at the time of the incident — to contact investigators. The NYPD noted that community cooperation is critical to constructing a comprehensive and legally sound account of the events leading up to and immediately following the shooting.

Investigation Ongoing

Detectives believe the fatal shooting was connected to an escalating gang-related dispute between rival factions operating in the area. Specifically, law enforcement sources indicated that investigators are examining whether the conflict involves members affiliated with the Gangster Disciples and a local street crew identified as the 59 Primetime Brims, active in and around the Coney Island neighborhood — an area referred to by local residents and community workers as "Koneysides." Officials cautioned that all such characterizations remain preliminary and subject to revision as the investigation develops further.

Authorities are also examining whether Simons himself may have intended to confront or target rival individuals prior to the shooting, according to sources with direct knowledge of the investigation. Police allege that the circumstances immediately preceding the incident suggest the encounter may not have been entirely unprovoked, though investigators stressed that this line of inquiry does not alter the legal framework under which the two suspects are currently being held. All allegations remain unproven in a court of law, and the investigation into the full sequence of events remains active.

The NYPD's Gang Division has been formally notified and is providing support to Brooklyn North Homicide detectives as they work to establish the full scope of the incident and any broader criminal network implications. Authorities declined to comment on specific intelligence gathered through ongoing gang-related investigations, citing the active nature of the case and potential legal sensitivities connected to the forthcoming prosecution.

Neighborhood Reaction

Shamar Simons Photo of Shamar Simons — A second photo of Simons, provided to NYC Daily Report, shows the 17-year-old Brooklyn resident prior to the incident. (NYC Daily Report)

The shooting drew expressions of deep grief and frustration from residents of the Coney Island Houses and the surrounding blocks. Neighbors described seeing police tape stretched across sidewalks and a heavy patrol presence throughout the late afternoon and into the evening hours. Several dozen individuals gathered near the police perimeter following news of Simons' death, many visibly shaken, some embracing one another in front of the barriers as detectives continued their work nearby.

Local community advocates expressed deep concern over the continuing cycle of violence affecting the neighborhood's young people. "Every time something like this happens, it is a reminder that we need more resources, more intervention, and more support for the families living here," said one community organizer who requested anonymity. "This community is tired of grieving its young people."

Elected officials representing the district were briefed on the shooting Tuesday evening and issued statements calling for accountability and urging community members to cooperate with investigators. The Brooklyn District Attorney's office confirmed it is working in close coordination with the NYPD on the case and indicated it would have more to say once formal charges have been filed.

Neighborhood Safety Concerns

Tuesday's fatal shooting is not the first violent incident to strike the Coney Island Houses complex in recent months. Residents and local advocates have long raised concerns about elevated tension in the area, attributing much of it to longstanding rivalries between street factions that, according to law enforcement, have resulted in a series of confrontations over the past year. Community members have repeatedly called on city officials to increase the presence of social workers, mental health professionals, and violence-interrupter programs in and around the development.

In response to Tuesday's shooting, the NYPD announced it would bolster patrols in the 60th Precinct, particularly around the Coney Island Houses complex. "We are increasing our presence in the area and are working alongside community partners to address the underlying tensions that contribute to these situations," an NYPD spokesperson told NYC Daily Report Tuesday evening.

Background Information

The Coney Island Houses — a New York City Housing Authority development situated in southern Brooklyn — is home to several thousand residents and comprises a cluster of high-rise residential buildings and ground-level common areas. The development has historically been subject to elevated levels of street violence, according to NYPD precinct data, though city officials and community organizations have made sustained efforts to reduce crime through outreach and investment in youth programming.

Law enforcement sources describe the Gangster Disciples and the 59 Primetime Brims as two factions that have maintained a contentious presence in parts of the Coney Island corridor for an extended period. Detectives believe the dispute underlying Tuesday's shooting predates the incident by several months. Authorities declined to specify the nature of the original conflict, citing the active investigation and the need to preserve the integrity of any future prosecution.

What Comes Next

The Medical Examiner's office is expected to formally determine the cause and manner of death in the coming days. In the interim, detectives are continuing to process physical evidence, review surveillance footage from multiple camera sources, and follow up on leads developed through witness interviews conducted at and near the scene. The firearm recovered by officers is expected to undergo ballistic testing, the results of which could prove significant in forthcoming legal proceedings against both suspects.

The Brooklyn District Attorney's office is expected to present the case to a grand jury in due course, pending the completion of the NYPD's investigation. Formal charges against Bennett and Brooks had not been publicly filed as of Tuesday evening, though sources indicated arraignment proceedings were anticipated within the standard 24-to-72-hour window following arrest. Both men are currently being held pending those proceedings.

The NYPD has asked anyone with information related to the shooting to contact the Crime Stoppers hotline at 1-800-577-TIPS (8477). Anonymous tips may also be submitted online at nypdcrimestoppers.com or via text message to 274637 (CRIMES), followed by TIP577. The case remains open. Simons is survived by his family, whose wishes for privacy are being respected during this difficult time. This story will continue to be updated as additional information becomes available.
